# FAQ: customer credit

## How is a customer's balance worked out?

The balance is the sum of the customer's credit ledger: credit purchases add to it, payments reduce it.

## When is a bill overdue?

Each credit purchase is due 30 days after the purchase date (POL-CREDIT-001 v2). After that it is overdue.

## A customer is over the limit but wants to buy on credit. What do we do?

Politely offer cash or UPI. Only the owner can allow credit above the limit.

## A customer's limit is Rs 5,000 but the policy says Rs 3,000. Which applies?

The Rs 5,000 limit was set under the old version 1 policy. It stays on record until the owner reviews it, but new credit that takes a household balance above Rs 3,000 needs the owner's approval.

## The balance is negative. What does that mean?

The ledger shows the customer paid more than they owed. This usually means a payment was entered twice. Follow SOP-DEDUP-001.

## How often can we remind a customer?

At most once every 7 days, and only after the bill is 7 days past due (POL-REMINDER-001).
