# Shopkeeper FAQ: using ORM_AI

## What can I ask ORM_AI?

Questions about your own shop: stock levels, what to reorder, sales for a day or a week, a customer's credit balance, open supplier orders, and what a shop policy says.

## Can ORM_AI see other shops' records?

No. Each shop sees only its own customers, products and sales. Suppliers are shared, so every shop sees the same supplier list.

## Will ORM_AI do things on its own?

It can look things up and send payment reminders that follow the reminder policy. Purchase orders, price changes, stock adjustments, refunds and credit changes are drafted for a person to confirm or approve (POL-APPROVAL-001).

## How do I know an answer is right?

Every answer names the records and policies it used. If ORM_AI cannot find something, it says so instead of guessing.

## What if ORM_AI is wrong?

Reject or edit the proposal in the Approvals page and tell the owner. Nothing that moves money or stock happens without a person.
