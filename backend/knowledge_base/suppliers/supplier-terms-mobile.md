# Supplier terms: mobile accessories

## TechLink Accessories Wholesale (SUP-011)

- Supplies: cables, earphones, earbuds, speakers, screen guards, covers, small accessories.
- Delivery: 6 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 30 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 3,000.
- Warranty: earbuds, neckbands and speakers carry a 6-month replacement warranty. Return defective units with the shop's bill copy; TechLink replaces them on the next delivery.

## Volt Mobile Distributors (SUP-012)

- Supplies: chargers, power banks, memory cards, pen drives.
- Delivery: 4 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 15 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 2,000.
- Warranty: chargers and power banks carry a 6-month replacement warranty.
