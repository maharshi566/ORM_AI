# Supplier terms: hardware, paint and plumbing

## Deccan Hardware Distributors (SUP-004)

- Supplies: cement, fasteners, binding wire, electrical items, tools.
- Delivery: 5 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 30 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 5,000.
- Cement is delivered by truck; orders under 50 bags may wait for the next truck to the area.

## Colorline Paints Depot (SUP-005)

- Supplies: paints, putty, brushes, rollers, sandpaper.
- Delivery: 4 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 30 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 5,000.
- Unopened paint cans can be returned within 30 days for credit.

## Pragati Pipes & Fittings (SUP-006)

- Supplies: PVC pipes, fittings, taps, valves, teflon tape.
- Delivery: 3 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 15 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 2,000.
