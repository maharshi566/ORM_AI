# Supplier terms: stationery and paper

## Saraswati Book Depot (SUP-007)

- Supplies: notebooks, pens, pencils, art supplies, instruments.
- Delivery: 5 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 30 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 1,500.
- School-season orders (June and January) should be placed 3 weeks ahead.

## PaperWorld Wholesale (SUP-008)

- Supplies: copier paper, chart paper, covers, office supplies.
- Delivery: 2 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 15 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 1,000.
- Short deliveries are credited on the next invoice when reported the same day with the delivery note.
