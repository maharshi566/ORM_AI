# Supplier terms: grocery and household goods

## Shree Balaji Traders (SUP-001)

- Supplies: rice, flour, sugar, salt, breakfast items, eggs.
- Delivery: 2 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 15 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 2,000.
- Rate changes are announced on the invoice.

## Annapurna Foods Distributors (SUP-002)

- Supplies: pulses, cooking oil, spices.
- Delivery: 3 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 7 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 1,500.
- Oil prices change often; check every invoice.

## Metro FMCG Agency (SUP-003)

- Supplies: tea, coffee, biscuits, noodles, soap, detergent, toothpaste, household items.
- Delivery: 4 days after the order.
- Payment terms: 30 days from delivery.
- Minimum order: Rs 3,000.
- Damaged packets are replaced on the next visit if reported within 2 days.
