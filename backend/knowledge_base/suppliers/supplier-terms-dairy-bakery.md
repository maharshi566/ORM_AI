# Supplier terms: dairy and bakery

## Nandi Milk Co-operative (SUP-009)

- Supplies: milk, curd, paneer, butter, ghee, cheese, buttermilk, eggs.
- Delivery: next morning for orders placed by 7 pm.
- Payment terms: 7 days.
- Minimum order: Rs 500.
- Unsold milk pouches are not taken back. Order to expected demand.
- Ghee and butter prices are revised by the co-operative; the new rate appears on the invoice.

## Golden Crust Bakery Supplies (SUP-010)

- Supplies: bread, pav, rusk, cakes, cookies, puffs.
- Delivery: next morning.
- Payment terms: 7 days.
- Minimum order: Rs 500.
- Bread unsold by its best-before date can be returned for 50% credit within one day.
