# Shop profile: Jai Hind Hardware & Sanitary (SHOP-042)

## Basics

- Type: hardware, paint and plumbing.
- Owner: Anjali Deshpande. Staff: Geetha Nambiar.
- Location: Mill Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 9:30 am to 8:00 pm, closed on Mondays.
- Open since: 2010.

## House rules

- Registered contractors may buy on credit up to Rs 15,000 and households up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Cement is the top seller. Keep at least 20 bags; order full truckloads of 80 bags from Deccan Hardware Distributors.
- Small fittings and fasteners are counted every month because they go missing easily.
- Contractors' monthly accounts are settled on the last day of each month.
