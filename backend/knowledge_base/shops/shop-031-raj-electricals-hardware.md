# Shop profile: Raj Electricals & Hardware (SHOP-031)

## Basics

- Type: hardware, paint and plumbing.
- Owner: Deepa Chandran. Staff: Kishore Babu.
- Location: Bus Stand Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 8:30 am to 7:30 pm, closed on Mondays.
- Open since: 2016.

## House rules

- Registered contractors may buy on credit up to Rs 15,000 and households up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Paint is ordered from Colorline Paints Depot. Demand rises before festivals (September to November).
- Small fittings and fasteners are counted every month because they go missing easily.
- Orders above Rs 300 are delivered by tempo within 1 km; a driver charge applies beyond that.
