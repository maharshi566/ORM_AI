# Shop profile: Sai Ram Provision Store (SHOP-011)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Harish Kulkarni. Staff: Vinay Naik.
- Location: Rajaji Nagar, Demo City.
- Hours: 7:00 am to 9:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2012.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Eggs are ordered in trays of 30 along with the rice and flour order.
- Home delivery within 1 km for bills above Rs 500; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
- Packets are checked for best-before dates on the first day of every month.
