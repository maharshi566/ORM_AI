# Shop profile: Morning Fresh Dairy (SHOP-049)

## Basics

- Type: dairy and bakery.
- Owner: Girija Prasad. Staff: Faisal Mir.
- Location: Hospital Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 6:30 am to 10:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2023.

## House rules

- Monthly milk accounts have a Rs 2,000 limit and are settled by the 5th of each month.
- Order milk and bread every evening for the next morning.
- Birthday cakes are made to order: take half the amount in advance and note it on the bill.
- Milk for monthly account customers is delivered within 2 km before 8:00 am.
