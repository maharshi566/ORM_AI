# Shop profile: Mahalakshmi Departmental Store (SHOP-026)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Satish Naik. Staff: Tejas Kamath.
- Location: Station Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 7:30 am to 10:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2015.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Rice, atta and sugar are ordered together from Shree Balaji Traders every Tuesday.
- Home delivery within 1 km for bills above Rs 500; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
- Before Diwali and Pongal keep double stock of sugar, oil and dal.
