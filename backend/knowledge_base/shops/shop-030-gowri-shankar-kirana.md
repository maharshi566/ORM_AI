# Shop profile: Gowri Shankar Kirana (SHOP-030)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Narayana Swamy. Staff: Sushma Naidu.
- Location: Temple Street, Demo City.
- Hours: 6:30 am to 9:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2009.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Cooking oil and pulses are counted every week because they are high-value and fast-moving.
- Home delivery within 1 km for bills above Rs 400; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
- Packets are checked for best-before dates on the first day of every month.
