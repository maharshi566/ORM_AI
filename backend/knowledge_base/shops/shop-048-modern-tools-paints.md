# Shop profile: Modern Tools & Paints (SHOP-048)

## Basics

- Type: hardware, paint and plumbing.
- Owner: Sunil Biswas. Staff: Rukmini Bai.
- Location: Station Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 8:30 am to 7:30 pm, closed on Sundays.
- Open since: 2013.

## House rules

- Registered contractors may buy on credit up to Rs 15,000 and households up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Cement is the top seller. Keep at least 20 bags; order full truckloads of 80 bags from Deccan Hardware Distributors.
- Paint is ordered from Colorline Paints Depot. Demand rises before festivals (September to November).
- Contractors' monthly accounts are settled on the 10th of each month.
