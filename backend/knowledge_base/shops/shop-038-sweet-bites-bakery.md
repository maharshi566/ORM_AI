# Shop profile: Sweet Bites Bakery (SHOP-038)

## Basics

- Type: dairy and bakery.
- Owner: Kamala Devi. Staff: Padma Priya.
- Location: Gandhi Nagar, Demo City.
- Hours: 6:30 am to 9:30 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2022.

## House rules

- Monthly milk accounts have a Rs 2,000 limit and are settled by the 5th of each month.
- Order milk and bread every evening for the next morning.
- Bakery items still unsold after two days are written off as damage.
- Milk for monthly account customers is delivered within 1 km before 8:00 am.
