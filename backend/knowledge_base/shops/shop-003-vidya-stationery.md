# Shop profile: Vidya Stationery & Xerox (SHOP-003)

## Basics

- Type: stationery, school supplies and photocopying.
- Owner: Anita Sharma. Staff: Rahul Das.
- Location: College Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 8:30 am to 8:30 pm, closed on Sundays.

## House rules

- Schools and tuition centres may buy on credit up to Rs 10,000 and pay monthly.
- Copier paper is used for the shop's own photocopying as well as sold, so never let it run out.
- Students do not get credit.
