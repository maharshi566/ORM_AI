# Shop profile: City Mobile Accessories (SHOP-010)

## Basics

- Type: mobile phone accessories.
- Owner: Gurpreet Singh. Staff: Jaspreet Kaur.
- Location: Bazaar Lane, Demo City.
- Hours: 10:30 am to 9:30 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2022.

## House rules

- Credit only for repair shops that buy regularly, up to Rs 5,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Defective items within 7 days are replaced from stock and returned to the supplier under warranty (POL-RETURNS-001).
- Cables and chargers are counted every week because they are small and easy to lose.
- Warranty cards are stapled to the bill for earbuds, power banks and speakers.
