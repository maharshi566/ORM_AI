# Shop profile: Student's Choice Stationery (SHOP-009)

## Basics

- Type: stationery, school supplies and photocopying.
- Owner: Farida Begum. Staff: Salman Ansari.
- Location: College Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 9:00 am to 8:00 pm, closed on Sundays.
- Open since: 2017.

## House rules

- Schools and tuition centres may buy on credit up to Rs 10,000 and pay monthly; families known to the owner up to Rs 2,000. Students do not get credit.
- June (schools reopen) and February to March (exams) are the busy periods: order notebooks and pens a month early.
- Photocopies cost Rs 2 a page.
- Calculators, geometry boxes and staplers are counted every month.
