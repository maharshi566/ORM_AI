# Shop profile: Shree Dairy Point (SHOP-024)

## Basics

- Type: dairy and bakery.
- Owner: Ganesh Pillai. Staff: Arvind Nair.
- Location: Lake View Colony, Demo City.
- Hours: 6:00 am to 9:30 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2017.

## House rules

- Monthly milk accounts have a Rs 2,000 limit and are settled by the 5th of each month.
- Order milk and bread every evening for the next morning.
- Record expired items as damage at closing every day.
- Bakery items still unsold after two days are written off as damage.
