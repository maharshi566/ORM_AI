# Shop profile: Venkateshwara Supermart (SHOP-020)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Nirmala Devi. Staff: Sudha Rao.
- Location: Gandhi Nagar, Demo City.
- Hours: 7:00 am to 9:30 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2011.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Home delivery within 2 km for bills above Rs 400; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
- Before Diwali and Pongal keep double stock of sugar, oil and dal.
- Packets are checked for best-before dates on the first day of every month.
