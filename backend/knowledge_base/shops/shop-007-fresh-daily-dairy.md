# Shop profile: Fresh Daily Dairy (SHOP-007)

## Basics

- Type: dairy and bakery.
- Owner: Lakshmi Prasad. Staff: Naveen Kumar.
- Location: Teachers Colony, Demo City.
- Hours: 6:30 am to 10:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2020.

## House rules

- Monthly milk accounts have a Rs 2,000 limit and are settled by the 5th of each month.
- Record expired items as damage at closing every day.
- Bakery items still unsold after two days are written off as damage.
- Milk for monthly account customers is delivered within 2 km before 8:00 am.
