# Shop profile: Gyan Ganga Books & Stationery (SHOP-021)

## Basics

- Type: stationery, school supplies and photocopying.
- Owner: Kiran Parikh. Staff: Vikram Patil.
- Location: Hospital Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 9:30 am to 8:30 pm, closed on Sundays.
- Open since: 2016.

## House rules

- Schools and tuition centres may buy on credit up to Rs 10,000 and pay monthly; families known to the owner up to Rs 2,000. Students do not get credit.
- Copier paper is also used for the shop's own photocopying, so never let it run out.
- Calculators, geometry boxes and staplers are counted every month.
- Bulk orders from schools go out with a delivery challan and are billed at month end.
