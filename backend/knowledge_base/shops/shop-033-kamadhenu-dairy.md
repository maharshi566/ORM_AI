# Shop profile: Kamadhenu Dairy (SHOP-033)

## Basics

- Type: dairy and bakery.
- Owner: Shabana Parveen. Staff: Nasreen Taj.
- Location: Old Town, Demo City.
- Hours: 6:30 am to 10:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2014.

## House rules

- Monthly milk accounts have a Rs 2,000 limit and are settled by the 5th of each month.
- Order milk and bread every evening for the next morning.
- Bakery items still unsold after two days are written off as damage.
- The chiller temperature is checked every morning before opening.
