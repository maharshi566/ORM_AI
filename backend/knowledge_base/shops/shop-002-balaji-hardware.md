# Shop profile: Balaji Hardware & Paints (SHOP-002)

## Basics

- Type: hardware, paint and plumbing.
- Owner: Suresh Patel. Staff: Manoj Yadav.
- Location: Station Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 9:00 am to 8:30 pm, closed on Sundays.

## House rules

- Registered contractors may buy on credit up to Rs 15,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Cement is the top seller. Keep at least 20 bags; order full truckloads of 80 bags.
- Painting demand rises before festivals (September to November).
