# Shop profile: Bharat Paints & Hardware (SHOP-014)

## Basics

- Type: hardware, paint and plumbing.
- Owner: Meenakshi Iyer. Staff: Swathi Reddy.
- Location: Station Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 9:00 am to 8:30 pm, closed on Sundays.
- Open since: 2015.

## House rules

- Registered contractors may buy on credit up to Rs 15,000 and households up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Small fittings and fasteners are counted every month because they go missing easily.
- Orders above Rs 400 are delivered by tempo within 2 km; a driver charge applies beyond that.
- Electrical items are tested at the counter before the sale.
