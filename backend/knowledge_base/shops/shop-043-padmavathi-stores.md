# Shop profile: Padmavathi Stores (SHOP-043)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Sridhar Acharya. Staff: Varun Menon.
- Location: Green Park, Demo City.
- Hours: 7:00 am to 9:00 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2014.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Rice, atta and sugar are ordered together from Shree Balaji Traders every Monday.
- Home delivery within 1 km for bills above Rs 300; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
- Packets are checked for best-before dates on the first day of every month.
