# Shop profile: Maruti Provision Mart (SHOP-039)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Ismail Qureshi. Staff: Aslam Pasha.
- Location: Canal Road, Demo City.
- Hours: 7:30 am to 9:30 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2012.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Cooking oil and pulses are counted every week because they are high-value and fast-moving.
- Eggs are ordered in trays of 30 along with the rice and flour order.
- Home delivery within 3 km for bills above Rs 400; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
