# Shop profile: Annapurna General Stores (SHOP-006)

## Basics

- Type: kirana (grocery and daily needs).
- Owner: Venkatesh Gowda. Staff: Pooja Shenoy.
- Location: Lake View Colony, Demo City.
- Hours: 7:30 am to 9:30 pm, every day.
- Open since: 2016.

## House rules

- Credit only for families known to the owner, up to Rs 3,000 (POL-CREDIT-001 v2).
- Rice, atta and sugar are ordered together from Shree Balaji Traders every Monday.
- Home delivery within 3 km for bills above Rs 400; no deliveries after 8:00 pm.
- A UPI QR code is at the counter; cards are accepted for bills above Rs 500.
