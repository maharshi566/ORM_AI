# Receiving supplier deliveries

## 1. Check against the order

Match every delivery with its purchase order number. Count each item before signing the delivery note.

## 2. Check condition

Check expiry dates on food and dairy. Check seals on electronics. Check cement bags for moisture and tears.

## 3. Record what arrived

Record the quantity actually received for each item. If everything arrived, the order becomes "received". If anything is missing, it becomes "partially received" and you follow SOP-SHORT-001.

## 4. Rates

If the invoice rate differs from the order, record the new cost price and follow SOP-PRICE-001.
