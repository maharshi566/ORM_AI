# Day-end closing checklist

## 1. Sales and money

1. Make sure every bill of the day is entered.
2. Count the cash. Compare cash and UPI totals with the day's sales (POL-CASH-001).
3. Check every credit payment has its mode and, for UPI, its reference.

## 2. Stock

1. Record expired dairy and bakery items as damage.
2. Look at products at or below their reorder level and draft orders (POL-REORDER-001).

## 3. Deliveries and closing

1. Check for deliveries expected today that did not arrive (POL-SUPPLIER-001).
2. Lock the cash box and switch off the billing computer.
