# Updating prices when a supplier changes rates

## 1. Record the new cost

Enter the new cost price from the supplier's invoice, with the supplier's name in the reason.

## 2. Work out the new selling price

1. New price = new cost plus the product's usual margin (10% to 25%, see POL-PRICING-001).
2. Round to the nearest rupee.
3. If the result is above MRP, use MRP and check the margin is still at least 8%.
4. If even MRP gives less than 8% margin, do not change the price yourself. Tell the owner.

## 3. Approval

Send the proposed price to the owner. Change the price only after approval, and record who approved it.

## 4. Time limit

Finish within 2 working days of receiving the new rate.
