# Monthly stock count procedure

## 1. When

On the last working day of each month, after closing. Fast-moving items (milk, bread, rice, sugar, cement) may also be counted weekly.

## 2. Steps

1. Print or open the product list with current stock.
2. Count each product on the shelf and in the back store. Count unopened cartons by the number inside.
3. Write the counted quantity next to the recorded quantity.
4. For any difference, recount once, check deliveries received today, and check bills not yet entered.
5. Record each remaining difference as an adjustment with the reason "Monthly stock count".

## 3. Approval

Adjustments follow POL-ADJUST-001: over Rs 1,000 at cost price needs the owner.

## 4. Follow-up

A shortfall on the same product twice in 3 months becomes a case for the owner.
