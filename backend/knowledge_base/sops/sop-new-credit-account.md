# Opening a credit account for a customer

## 1. Who can open one

Only the owner.

## 2. Checks

- The customer has bought from the shop for at least 3 months.
- The customer's name and phone number are correct and not already on another record.
- The customer understands the 30-day payment term.

## 3. Set the limit

Use the standard limit for the customer type in POL-CREDIT-001 version 2. A higher limit is the owner's decision and must be noted with a reason.

## 4. Record it

Mark the customer as allowed credit, set the limit, and add a note with the date and the owner's name.
