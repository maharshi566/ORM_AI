# Handling short or damaged supplier deliveries

## 1. At the time of delivery

- Write the missing or damaged quantity on the supplier's delivery note and ask the delivery person to sign it.
- Take a photo of damaged items.

## 2. Record it

- Record only the quantity actually received in good condition.
- Open a case with the purchase order number, the supplier and the missing quantity.

## 3. Same day

Message the supplier with the order number and the missing quantity. Ask for either:

- the missing items on the next delivery, or
- a credit note for the missing items against the next invoice.

## 4. Do not reorder twice

Do not place a fresh order for the missing items until the supplier replies, unless the item will run out before the supplier's next delivery. In that case, ask the owner.

## 5. Close the case

Close the case when the items or the credit note arrive. Repeated short deliveries from one supplier go to the owner for review (POL-SUPPLIER-001).
