# Handling duplicate customers and duplicate entries

## 1. Duplicate customer records

Two records with the same phone number are probably the same person.

1. Compare the names, localities and purchase history.
2. Ask the customer at their next visit.
3. The owner approves merging them. The older record is kept and the newer one deactivated.

Never merge records without the owner's approval.

## 2. Duplicate payment entries

Two payments with the same UPI reference are probably one payment entered twice.

1. Check the UPI statement for the reference.
2. Open a case and tell the owner.
3. After the owner approves, add an adjustment entry that cancels the duplicate. Never delete the original rows (POL-CASH-001).
