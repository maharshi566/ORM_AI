# Cash and UPI reconciliation policy

## 1. Daily closing

At closing, compare the day's cash and UPI totals with the sales list (SOP-DAYEND-001).

## 2. Recording customer payments

Record every credit payment with its mode. For UPI, write the UPI reference number in the note.

## 3. Duplicate entries

The same UPI reference must appear only once in the credit ledger. Two entries with the same reference are a probable duplicate. Do not delete either entry. Open a case and ask the owner, who approves a correcting adjustment after checking the bank statement.

## 4. Corrections

Never delete or edit a ledger row. Correct mistakes with a new adjustment entry that the owner approves, and note the reason.
