# Stock adjustment and damage policy

## 1. Every change has a reason

Every stock change that is not a sale, a delivery or a customer return must be recorded as an adjustment or as damage, with a reason.

## 2. Count before adjusting

Follow SOP-COUNT-001. Recount once before recording any shortfall. Check the back store and recent deliveries first.

## 3. Approval

- Adjustments worth up to Rs 1,000 at cost price: a staff member confirms.
- Adjustments worth over Rs 1,000 at cost price: the owner approves.

## 4. Repeated shortfalls

If the same product is short at two counts within 3 months, open a case and tell the owner. Check sales records, deliveries and returns for the period between the counts.

## 5. Damage and expiry

Dairy and bakery items past their best-before date are recorded as damage every evening. Other damaged goods are recorded when found.
