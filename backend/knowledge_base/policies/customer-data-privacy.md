# Customer data and privacy policy

## 1. What we keep

Name, phone number, locality, purchases and the credit balance. Nothing else.

## 2. Who may see it

Only the shop owner and staff of the same shop. Another shop using ORM_AI cannot see our customers.

## 3. Sharing

Never tell anyone else a customer's balance, phone number or purchases, including family members, unless the customer has said so.

## 4. Messages

Send messages only about the customer's own account. No bulk promotional messages.

## 5. Removing data

When a customer with no balance asks to be removed, the owner deactivates the record.
