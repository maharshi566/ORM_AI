# Payment reminder policy

How and when we remind customers about unpaid credit.

## 1. When to send

- Send the first reminder when a bill is 7 days past its due date.
- Send at most one reminder to the same customer every 7 days.
- Send only between 9:00 am and 8:00 pm.

## 2. How to send

Use WhatsApp first. Use SMS if the customer has no WhatsApp. Use the customer's own phone number only.

## 3. What to say

Be polite and short. Use this template:

> Namaste {name}, a gentle reminder from {shop}: Rs {amount} is pending on your account since {date}. Please pay when convenient. Thank you.

Never threaten, never shame, and never mention the amount to anyone except the customer.

## 4. When not to send

- The customer has disputed the balance (POL-CREDIT-001, section 7).
- The customer paid in the last 3 days, even partly.
- A reminder was sent in the last 7 days.

## 5. After three reminders

If three reminders have gone unanswered, stop sending them. The owner calls the customer personally and decides what to do next.
