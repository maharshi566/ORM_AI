# Customer credit (udhaar) policy — version 2

Applies to all shops from 1 April 2026. It replaces version 1.

## 1. Who can buy on credit

A customer can buy on credit only when all of these are true:

- The customer is in the customer list with a working phone number.
- The customer has bought from the shop for at least 3 months.
- The owner has opened a credit account for them (SOP-CREDIT-OPEN-001).

Staff and ORM_AI may not open new credit accounts.

## 2. Credit limits

| Customer type | Standard limit |
| --- | --- |
| Household | Rs 3,000 |
| Household with a monthly milk account (dairy shop) | Rs 2,000 |
| Small business | Rs 5,000 |
| School or institution | Rs 10,000 |
| Registered contractor (hardware shop) | Rs 15,000 |

Any limit above the standard needs the owner's approval. Limits set under version 1 (often Rs 5,000 for households) stay on the record until the owner reviews them, but new credit that takes a household balance above Rs 3,000 needs the owner's approval.

## 3. Payment terms

Each credit purchase is due 30 days after the purchase date.

## 4. When credit is blocked

Do not give new credit when either of these is true:

- The new bill would take the balance above the customer's limit.
- Any bill is more than 15 days past its due date (45 days after purchase).

Tell the customer politely and offer cash or UPI instead. Only the owner can make an exception.

## 5. Reminders

Reminders follow POL-REMINDER-001.

## 6. Limit changes and write-offs

Only the owner can raise or lower a limit, or write off an unpaid balance. ORM_AI may suggest these, with evidence, but must send them for the owner's approval.

## 7. Disputes

If a customer says the balance is wrong, stop reminders for that customer, open a case, and check the UPI statement and the cash book before changing anything (POL-CASH-001).
