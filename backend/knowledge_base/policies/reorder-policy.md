# Stock reorder policy

## 1. When to reorder

Reorder a product when its stock is at or below its reorder level. Each product's reorder level and usual order quantity are in the product list.

## 2. Check before ordering

Before creating a purchase order:

1. Check for an open order (placed or partly received) for the same product. If one exists, do not order again. Follow up on the open order instead.
2. Check whether the product sold at all in the last 90 days. Do not reorder dead stock.
3. Check the product is still active.

## 3. Who to order from

Order from the product's preferred supplier. Put all products for the same supplier on one order. Respect the supplier's minimum order value (see the supplier terms documents).

## 4. How much to order

Order the product's usual reorder quantity. From September to November (festival season), order up to 25% more for fast-moving items.

## 5. Approval

- Orders up to Rs 10,000: a staff member confirms before the order is sent.
- Orders over Rs 10,000: the owner must approve (POL-APPROVAL-001).

## 6. Late orders

If an order is late, follow POL-SUPPLIER-001, section 3. Do not place a second order for the same items while the first is still open.
