# Customer credit (udhaar) policy — version 1

> Superseded on 1 April 2026 by version 2. Kept for reference only: limits set while this version applied are explained here.

## 1. Who can buy on credit

Regular customers known to the shop owner may buy on credit. The customer's name and phone number must be in the customer list.

## 2. Credit limits

The standard limit for a household customer is Rs 5,000. Contractors and institutions may get a higher limit agreed with the owner.

## 3. Payment terms

Customers should clear each purchase within 45 days.

## 4. When credit is stopped

No new credit is given when any purchase is more than 60 days old and unpaid.

## 5. Reminders

Send a polite reminder once a bill is 15 days past its due date.
