# Pricing and discount policy

## 1. Never above MRP

The selling price may never be higher than the MRP printed on the product.

## 2. Margin

- Target margin: 10% to 25% above cost price, depending on the category.
- Minimum margin: 8% above cost price.
- Never sell below cost price, except for an approved clearance sale.

## 3. When a supplier raises the cost

Update the selling price within 2 working days (SOP-PRICE-001). If the new cost means the minimum margin cannot be reached without going above MRP, tell the owner. The owner decides between selling at MRP with a lower margin, finding another supplier, or stopping the product.

## 4. Discounts

- Staff may give up to 5% off a bill above Rs 500.
- The owner may give up to 10%.
- Larger discounts, and clearance prices for dead stock, need the owner's approval.

## 5. Approval

Every price change needs the owner's approval (POL-APPROVAL-001).
