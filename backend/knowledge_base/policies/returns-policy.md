# Customer returns and refunds policy

## 1. Time limit

Customers may return items within 7 days of purchase, with the bill or a matching record in the sales list.

## 2. What can be returned

- Unused, sealed items in original packing: refund or exchange.
- Defective items (for example electronics that do not work): replace, or refund if no replacement is in stock.
- Food, dairy and bakery items: only if spoiled or damaged at the time of purchase.
- Paint mixed to order and cut lengths of pipe or wire: not returnable.

## 3. How to refund

Refund the same way the customer paid: cash for cash, UPI for UPI. For a credit purchase, reduce the customer's credit balance instead of paying out.

## 4. Approval

Refunds up to Rs 2,000 need a staff member's confirmation. Refunds over Rs 2,000 need the owner's approval.

## 5. Defective electronics

Return defective accessories to the supplier under their warranty terms (see the supplier terms for mobile accessories). Record the return to stock and the return to supplier separately.
